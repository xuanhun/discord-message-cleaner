Name: Discord Message Cleaner
Short description: Filter and preview Discord messages in a floating panel and open the native confirmation for each deletion.

Description
Open a Discord channel, direct message, or group chat in Chrome. Select the floating button to show or hide the cleaner.

• Browse chat history and filter messages by sender, date, text, regular expression, or attachments.
• Review matching messages and choose which message to open next.
• Confirm each deletion directly in Discord's original confirmation dialog.

The cleanup panel is currently in Simplified Chinese. The extension processes message data locally to support filtering and previewing. Discord controls deletion permissions. This independent extension is not affiliated with Discord.
